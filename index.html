Features
Everything a reader needs
📖

Always there

A resizable panel that floats over any webpage. Make it as small as a widget or as large as you like. It stays out of your way until you need it.

🤖

AI catch-up

Been away for a while? Hit one button and get a 2-sentence briefing on where you are in the story. Back in context instantly.

🔊

Listen while you work

Text-to-speech reads your book aloud so you can keep your eyes on your work. Multiple voices, sentence-level highlighting.

🔄

Picks up where you left off

Your position syncs across every tab and every device automatically. Switch from your laptop to your desktop — Folio remembers.
